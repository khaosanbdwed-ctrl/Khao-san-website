# Khao San website — how to manage it

This guide is for the Khao San team. It covers everything you can change on
the website yourselves, and how to prepare photos and videos so they look
right on phones, tablets and computers.

You do not need a developer to change words, photos, videos, dishes, outlets
or opening hours. You do need one for changes to the design itself (layout,
fonts, colours of the brand, animation).

---

## 1. Signing in

1. Go to **khaosan.bd/admin**.
2. Enter the username and password you were given.
3. You are signed in for up to 7 days on that browser. Press **Sign Out**
   (bottom of the left menu) when you finish on a shared computer.

**Keep the login safe.** This one login can change the whole website.

- Store the password in a password manager. Do not send it over WhatsApp or
  email, and do not write it on paper near the till.
- If someone who knew the password leaves, ask your developer to change it.
  Changing it signs everyone out everywhere.
- After 8 wrong passwords from one place, sign-in is blocked for 15 minutes.
  If that happens, wait 15 minutes and try again.

## 2. The dashboard

| Section | What you change there |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | The number of dishes and categories visitors actually see on the menu, which dishes are hidden, and links to everything else. |
| **Website Content** | Every word, photo, video, background and button on the site, the order of homepage sections, outlets and opening hours, the Privacy and Terms pages, and how the site looks on Google and when shared. |
| **Menu Control** | The dishes: names, descriptions, badges, photos (and prices, which are kept but not shown on the site). |
| **Media Library** | Every photo and video you have uploaded. Anything here can be reused anywhere. |

## 3. Changing the website (Website Content)

Website Content has seven areas: **Homepage, Menu page, Header, Footer,
Locations & hours, Privacy & Terms, Google & sharing.**

Open an area, then open a section (for example *Opening (hero)*). Every field
has a **Reset** link that puts back the original.

### How a change goes live

1. **Edit.** Nothing is saved yet; the badge says *Unsaved changes*.
2. **Save draft.** Saved, but the public website has not changed.
3. **Preview ↗.** Opens the real website in a new tab showing your draft.
   An orange bar at the bottom says you are in preview. Only you see this.
   Check it on your phone too: sign in on the phone and press Preview there.
4. **Publish.** Visitors see the change straight away.

**Made a mistake?**
- *Discard draft* throws away unpublished changes.
- *History* lists the last 40 published versions. **Restore** copies an old
  version into your draft; preview it, then Publish.
- *Reset all to original* puts the whole area back to how the website was
  designed. It only becomes live when you Publish.

> If you see *"Your session has expired"*, do not close the tab. Sign in again
> in a new tab, come back, and press **Save draft**. Your edits are kept.

### Show, hide and reorder homepage sections

On the Homepage area, each section has a **Shown / Hidden** switch and ↑ ↓
arrows. Hidden sections disappear from the site after you Publish; their
content is kept, so you can show them again later.

### Writing tips

- `*words in asterisks*` are set in the brand italic (e.g.
  `Bangkok, *quietly elevated.*`). This works in fields marked with the
  asterisk hint.
- In Privacy & Terms: leave an empty line between paragraphs, start a line
  with `- ` for a bullet point, and write `[Facebook](https://facebook.com/…)`
  for a link.
- `{outlet}` in the outlet WhatsApp message becomes the outlet's name.
  `{year}` in the footer copyright becomes the current year.
- Clearing a button's text removes the button.
- Short is better: headings are designed for 2–6 words.

### Buttons

Every button can either **open a page or web address** (`/menu`,
`/#locations`, or a full `https://…` address), **open a WhatsApp chat** with a
message already typed (sent to the main WhatsApp number in Locations &
hours), or **call a phone number**.

### Backgrounds

Every section has a Background panel. Leave it empty to keep the designed
background. Otherwise you can set:

- **Colour** — replaces the designed background.
- **Photo** and/or **Video** — over the colour.
- **Focus** — which part of the photo/video stays in view when the screen
  crops it (centre, top, bottom, left, right).
- **Darken / tint** — a wash over everything. Raise it (try 30–50%) when text
  is hard to read over a busy photo or video.
- **Text colour** — switch to *Light text* on dark backgrounds and *Dark
  text* on light backgrounds.

## 4. Photos and videos — sizes and shapes

**The short version:** upload straight from a phone or camera. Photos are
resized and compressed for you; videos get a computer version, a lighter
phone version and a still image, are trimmed to 20 seconds and have their
sound removed (background videos always play silently on a loop).

**Any shape fits any screen.** A photo or video always *fills* its frame and
the parts that do not fit are cropped — nothing is stretched or squashed.
So the one rule that matters most is: **keep the important part in the
middle**, away from the edges. Use the **Focus** setting if the wrong part is
being shown.

**Limits:** photos up to 10 MB, videos up to 50 MB, dish photos up to 15 MB.
Photo types: JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF. Video types: MP4, WebM.
For video, upload from a current **Chrome, Edge or Safari** so it can be
compressed; older browsers upload the original file (it works, but loads
slower for visitors).

### Recommended size for each place

| Where | Shape on screen | Upload | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Homepage opening (hero)** — full screen | Tall on phones (1:2), wide on computers (16:9) | **Square video 1080×1080** (or 16:9 at 1920×1080), 8–20 s | The same video fills a tall phone and a wide monitor. A square video keeps about half of its width on a phone and half of its height on a computer — the safest choice. With a wide 16:9 video, a phone shows only the **middle quarter**: keep the action in the centre. |
| **Menu page opening (hero)** | Tall on phones, 16:9 on computers | Same as above | Same advice. |
| **Theatre of Fire video** | 16:9 on phones/tablets, 3:2 on computers | **16:9 video, 1920×1080** (1280×720 is fine) | Keep the action in the centre. |
| **Rooms gallery** | Mixed: portrait on phones, about square on computers | **Portrait 4:5 photos, at least 1600×2000** | Shown in groups of three: one large, two small. Add photos in multiples of 3 for a tidy strip. Fill in the *Description* (for blind visitors and Google). |
| **Signature dishes** (homepage) | Square | **Square photo, at least 1200×1200** | Use the same style as the menu photos for consistency. |
| **Outlet photo** (Locations & hours) | Wide, about 16:10 | **Landscape, at least 1600×1000** | The room should be recognisable even when cropped slightly. |
| **Gift card images** | About 7:4 | **1524×871** PNG/WebP | The card artwork as designed. |
| **Brush stroke behind the gift cards** | — | Transparent PNG | Remove it to show the cards on their own. |
| **Section backgrounds** (photo) | Very tall on phones, wide on computers | **At least 2560 px wide** | Busy photos make text hard to read: add a Darken wash. Textures and soft patterns work better than detailed pictures. |
| **Footer background** | Very wide on computers (about 3.5:1) | **At least 2560 px wide**, texture | Keep it dark so the white logo and text stay readable (or turn off *Show the logo in white* and choose *Dark text*). |
| **Logo** (header, hero, footer) | — | **Transparent PNG/WebP, square-ish, at least 500 px** | Must have a see-through background. The opening (hero) and the footer show it as an all-white silhouette; the header shows it in its own colours. |
| **Picture when shared** (Google & sharing) | 1.91:1 | **1200×630** photo | What WhatsApp, Facebook and others show with a link. Saved as JPEG automatically (WhatsApp does not reliably show other formats). Keep important content in the centre; consider including the logo. |
| **Browser tab icon** | Square | **512×512 PNG** | A simple mark; detail disappears at small size. |

### Videos: good practice

- 8–20 seconds that loop smoothly (the end flows back to the start).
- Steady footage; slow movement looks premium, fast cuts feel busy.
- No text or logos burned into the video — they will be cropped on some screens.
- No sound needed (it is removed).
- The still image shown while a video loads is made for you. To use a
  different one, set *Still shown while the video loads*.

## 5. Dishes (Menu Control)

### Add a dish

1. **Menu Control → + Add New Dish.**
2. Fill in **Dish Name**, **Category** and **Price** (prices are kept for your
   records; the website does not show them).
3. Optional: **Portion note** — shown small after the name, e.g. `(6pcs)`.
4. **Description** — one or two sentences: what is in it and how it is cooked.
5. **Badges** — *Spicy*, *Signature*, *Chef's Special*, *New*. Use them
   sparingly; if every dish is "Signature", none is.
6. Optional: **Extra note** — a short line under the description.
7. **Photo** — see below. When the preview appears, press **Save Dish**.

The dish appears on the website immediately.

### Dish photos — the important part

Upload each dish **with its background removed** — a **PNG or WebP with a
see-through background**, the whole plate in view. The dashboard then places
it on the menu's warm studio backdrop with a soft shadow, sized and centred
exactly like every other dish, so the menu always looks consistent.

- At least **1500 px** wide; up to 15 MB.
- The whole plate in the picture, nothing cut off at the edges.
- Shoot every dish from the **same angle** (all top-down, or all at about
  45°) and in similar light.
- Remove backgrounds with Canva (*Background Remover*), remove.bg, or
  Photoshop, and export as **PNG**.
- A JPG cannot be see-through. If you upload a photo that still has its
  background, the dashboard says so and does not use it.

### Edit or delete

Use **Edit** / **Delete** on the dish's row. Search and the category filter
help find a dish quickly. Deleting removes the dish from the website at once.

### Hide or show a dish

To take a dish off the website for a while (out of season, sold out, not
photographed yet) without losing it, press **Hide** on its row. It disappears
from the public menu at once, but stays in Menu Control marked **Hidden from
website**, with its photo and details intact. Press **Show** to put it back.

Hidden dishes are not counted in the Overview's "Dishes on the menu" figure -
that number always matches what visitors see. The Overview lists them by name
under "Hidden from the menu".

> Categories (e.g. adding a new section like "Specials") and the "Add ons"
> lines under Noodles, Rice and Rice Bowls are set up by your developer.

## 6. Outlets and opening hours (Website Content → Locations & hours)

- Keep each outlet's **name, address and phone exactly the same as on Google
  Maps**. Google compares them; differences hurt your search ranking.
- **WhatsApp numbers**: digits only, with the country code and no `+`, e.g.
  `8801600068193`.
- **Opening hours**: tick the days, set opening and closing times. Add a
  second set of hours for days that differ (e.g. Friday). The footer, the
  homepage and Google's information all update together.
- **Main WhatsApp number** (top of the area) receives every "Contact us" and
  "Book a table" message.

## 7. Google and sharing (Website Content → Google & sharing)

- **Site title** — about 50–60 characters.
- **Description for Google** — about 150 characters; describe the restaurant
  and mention Gulshan, Dhanmondi and Uttara.
- **Picture when shared** — 1200×630.
- Google can take days or weeks to show changes; WhatsApp and Facebook may
  keep showing the old picture for a while.

---

## 8. Media Library

Everything you upload appears here and can be chosen from any photo or video
field (*Choose from library*). **Deleting a file removes it from every place
it is used**, so check before deleting. The website's original photos and
videos are built in and are not listed; use **Reset** on a field to bring one
back.

---

## 9. If something looks wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Your session has expired" | Sign in again in a new tab, then press Save draft in the original tab. |
| "Too many failed attempts" | Wait 15 minutes. |
| A change is not on the website | Did you press **Publish** (not just Save draft)? Then refresh the page. |
| Text is hard to read over a photo/video | Raise *Darken / tint* in that section's Background, or change *Text colour*. |
| The wrong part of a photo/video shows | Change *Focus* in the Background panel. |
| A dish photo was refused | It still has a background, or it is not PNG/WebP. Remove the background and export as PNG. |
| Upload is slow or fails | Use Wi-Fi; check the file size limits above. |
| Something is broken and you cannot fix it | Open **History** and **Restore** the last good version, then Publish. Then contact your developer. |

---

## 10. Things to check once a month

- Open the website on a phone and a computer; check a few pages and buttons.
- Check that the outlet details and hours still match Google Maps.
- Check that the WhatsApp buttons open a chat with the right number.
